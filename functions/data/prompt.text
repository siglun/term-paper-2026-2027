What Claude did:

Here’s the mördarsnigel data as TSV in search-interest-swedish-moerdarsnigel.tsv. It has 274 monthly rows from 2004-01 to 2026-10.

Time (real number): year + (day − 1) / days in that year. So 1 January is exactly 2004.0000, and leap years divide by 366. Values are given to 4 decimals.
Day number: day 1–366. Because 2004, 2008 and the other leap years have 29 February, every month from March onward falls one day later in those years. For example, 1 July is day 183 in 2024 but day 182 in 2025.
